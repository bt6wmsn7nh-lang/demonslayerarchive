Anime learning guide

Anime is Japanese animation. It includes many genres, visual styles, age ratings, and storytelling traditions—not just one type of show.

01 · Anime

What is anime?

Anime is animation produced in Japan or closely associated with Japanese animation culture. Series can be action, comedy, romance, mystery, sports, fantasy, and more.

01
02 · Manga

What is manga?

Manga means Japanese comics. Most Japanese manga pages are read from right to left. Demon Slayer began as manga before becoming an anime.

02
03 · Adaptation

Manga to anime

An anime adaptation turns drawn panels into timed scenes with acting, music, motion, color, editing, and sound effects.

03
04 · Genres

What is shonen?

Shonen is a publishing category traditionally aimed at younger male readers, but its stories are enjoyed by people of many ages and identities.

Core knowledge

The world at a glance

Hero

Tanjiro Kamado

A compassionate swordsman who joins the Corps after tragedy strikes his family. His extraordinary sense of smell helps him track enemies and understand danger.

Sister

Nezuko Kamado

Tanjiro's younger sister. Although transformed into a demon, she fights to protect humans and travels in a sunlight-blocking wooden box.

Organization

Demon Slayer Corps

A hidden organization of trained swordsmen and support teams who protect people from demons using Nichirin weapons and Breathing Styles.

Technique

Breathing Styles

Specialized breathing and swordsmanship systems that improve focus, speed, strength, endurance, and control.

Elite

The Hashira

The Corps' most accomplished swordsmen. Each Hashira represents a distinctive Breathing Style and combat philosophy.

Threat

Demons

Regenerating creatures created through Muzan's blood. They usually fear sunlight and may develop unique Blood Demon Arts.
